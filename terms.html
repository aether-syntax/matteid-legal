Privacy Policy

What MatteID does

MatteID helps you produce a document photograph that follows the requirements an issuing authority has published. It reads those requirements from the authority's own material, measures your photograph against the ones it can measure, and tells you plainly which it could not check.

What MatteID does not promise

MatteID is not a government app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any issuing authority. MatteID cannot guarantee that any photograph will be accepted. Acceptance is decided by the issuing authority, which may apply judgement, local practice or requirements it has not published. When a photograph passes, MatteID says it passed MatteID's own checks for the requirements that were verified — nothing more. Treat any claim of guaranteed approval as false, including from anyone reselling this app.

Where the requirements come from

Each document in the app links to its issuing authority's official website. Where a requirement was read from an official publication, the app names that publication and the date it was read; where it comes from a table compiled for MatteID instead, the app says so. Authorities change their rules and do not always announce it, and some publish nothing at all. Where a requirement is unverified, missing or disputed between two official sources, the app says so on the document's own row rather than filling the gap with a guess. Confirm the requirements with the issuing authority before you submit.
