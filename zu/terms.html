Umgomo Wobumfihlo

Lokho okwenziwa yi-MatteID

I-MatteID ikusiza ukuba wenze isithombe sencwadi esilandela izidingo ezishicilelwe yisiphathimandla esikhiphayo. Ifunda lezo zidingo ezintweni zesiphathimandla uqobo, ikale isithombe sakho ngalezo ekwazi ukuzikala, futhi ikutshele ngokucacile lezo engakwazanga ukuzihlola.

Lokho i-MatteID engakuthembisi

I-MatteID ayikwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuthi noma yisiphi isithombe sizokwamukelwa. Ukwamukelwa kuthathwa yisiphathimandla esikhiphayo, esingasebenzisa ukubona kwaso, inqubo yendawo noma izidingo ezingashicilelwanga. Lapho isithombe siphumelela, i-MatteID isho ukuthi siphumelele ekuhloleni kwayo ngokwayo kwezidingo eziqinisekisiwe — akukho okunye. Thatha noma yikuphi ukusho kokuvunyelwa okuqinisekisiwe njengokungelona iqiniso, kuhlanganise nanoma ubani othengisa lolu hlelo lokusebenza kabusha.

Lapho izidingo zivela khona

Zonke izidingo ezikuhlelo lokusebenza zibhalwe nomthombo osemthethweni ezifundwe kuwo kanye nosuku ezifundwe ngalo. Iziphathimandla ziyayishintsha imithetho yazo futhi azihlali zikwazisa lokho, kanti ezinye azishicileli lutho nhlobo. Lapho isidingo singaqinisekisiwe, singekho noma siphikiswa phakathi kwemithombo emibili esemthethweni, uhlelo lokusebenza lusho njalo kulowo mugqa wencwadi kunokugcwalisa isikhala ngokuqagela. Qinisekisa izidingo nesiphathimandla esikhiphayo ngaphambi kokuthumela.

Amakhredithi ezithombe

Ukugcina noma ukuphrinta isithombe esiqediwe kusebenzisa ikhredithi eyodwa. Ikhredithi idonswa kuphela ngemva kokuba ifayela selakhiwe futhi lahlolwa; ukukhipha okuhlulekile akubizi lutho futhi ukuzama kabusha kumahhala. Amakhredithi awanalo inani lemali yangempela, awaphelelwa yisikhathi, futhi angowe-akhawunti yesitolo ewathengile.

Izinkokhelo nokubuyiselwa kwemali

Zonke izinkokhelo zisingathwa yi-App Store noma i-Google Play ngaphansi kwemigomo yazo. Ukubuyiselwa kwemali kucelwa esitolo, hhayi kwa-Aether Syntax, ngoba asikaze siyiphathe inkokhelo.

Ukusebenzisa kahle uhlelo lokusebenza

Sebenzisa i-MatteID ukwenza isithombe sakho, noma somuntu onelungelo lokumthwebula njengomntwana wakho. Ungayisebenzisi ukukhohlisa ngokuthi ungubani noma ukwenza isithombe sencwadi engeyona eyakho. Unesibopho ngalokho okuthumela kwisiphathimandla.

Izithombe zakho zihlala zingezakho

Ugcina wonke amalungelo ezithombeni ozenzayo. I-Aether Syntax ayifuni layisense phezu kwazo futhi ayikwazi — azikaze zifike kithi.

Ukutholakala

I-MatteID isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi uma isifakiwe. Ukuthenga amakhredithi amasha kudinga i-inthanethi ngoba isitolo siyakudinga lokho. Singashintsha noma siyeke ukunikeza izici ezithile ezinguqulweni ezizayo; amakhredithi osuwanikeziwe ahlala esebenziseka.

Imikhawulo yesibopho

I-MatteID iyithuluzi lokulungisa isithombe, akusona iseluleko mayelana nesicelo. I-Aether Syntax ayinasibopho ngesicelo esinqatshiwe, isikhathi esinqunyiwe esiphuthiwe, imali ekhokhwe kwisiphathimandla, noma nanoma yikuphi okunye ukulahlekelwa okuvela esinqumweni esenziwa yisiphathimandla. Akukho lapha okunciphisa amalungelo onawo ngaphansi komthetho wezwe lakho angenakuncishiswa ngesivumelwano.
