Imigomo Yokusebenzisa

Ngamafuphi nje

Isithombe sakho senziwa, sihlolwe futhi sigcinwe ocingweni lwakho. Asilayishwa ndawo, futhi i-Aether Syntax ayisoze yasithola. Asikugcinele i-akhawunti futhi asinayo irekhodi yalokho okuthwebulile.

Izithombe zakho

Izithombe ozithathayo noma ozikhethayo zigcinwa endaweni engo-MatteID kuphela, ezingakwazi ukufundwa ngamanye izinhlelo zokusebenza ocingweni. Ukuthola ubuso bakho, ukukala, ukusika, ukushintsha ingemuva kanye nokuhlola umphumela ngokwezidingo zencwadi konke kwenzeka ocingweni kusetshenziswa izici zokubona ezakhelwe kulo. Asikho isithombe, ukukalwa kobuso noma ingxenye yesithombe ethunyelwa kwenye indawo ngenxa yanoma yikuphi kwalokhu.

Okugcinwa ocingweni lwakho

Esithombeni ngasinye osenzayo, i-MatteID igcina isithombe sokuqala, isithombe esiqediwe, isithombe esincane kanye nerekhodi yokuthi besenzelwe yiphi incwadi. Iphinde igcine irekhodi lamakhredithi ezithombe owathengile noma owasebenzisile. Konke kuhlala ocingweni. Ukususa isithombe ku-MatteID kususa amafayela aso; Izilungiselelo zinokukhetha okususa zonke izithombe ngesikhathi esisodwa.

Izimvume

I-MatteID icela ikhamera kuphela lapho uthatha isithombe, icela ilabhulali yakho yezithombe kuphela uma ukhetha isithombe esikhona, futhi icela imvume yokwengeza kulabhulali yakho yezithombe kuphela uma ugcina isithombe esiqediwe. Ayilufundi ilabhulali yakho yezithombe iyodwa, futhi ayiceli lutho olunye — ayiceli indawo okuyo, imakrofoni, noma oxhumana nabo.

Ukuthenga amakhredithi ezithombe

Amakhredithi ezithombe athengwa nge-App Store noma nge-Google Play. Isitolo yiso esisingatha inkokhelo bese sitshela i-MatteID ukuthi ikhredithi ivunyelwe; i-Aether Syntax ayilokothi ibone ikhadi lakho, ikheli lakho lokukhokha, noma i-akhawunti yakho yesitolo. Lokhu kuthenga yisikhathi sodwa lapho i-MatteID isebenzisa khona i-inthanethi. Izixhumanisi eziya kuwebhusayithi esemthethweni yesiphathimandla zivuleka kusiphequluli sakho sewebhu, futhi i-MatteID ayithumeli lutho uma uvula eyodwa.

Esingakuqoqi

Ayikho i-akhawunti, akukho ukungena ngemvume futhi alikho ikheli le-imeyili. Azikho izibalo zokusebenzisa, akukho ukubika amaphutha okufaka okuqukethwe kwakho, asikho isihlonzi sokukhangisa futhi akukho ukulandelela kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo. Asikho iphrofayili eyakhiwayo ngawe, ngoba akukho lutho mayelana nokusebenzisa kwakho uhlelo lokusebenza olufika kithi.

Ifayela oligcinayo

Isithombe esigcinwa yi-MatteID siphatha kuphela usayizi odingwa yincwadi. Indawo lapho isithombe sathathwa khona, isikhathi, uhlobo lwekhamera negama lesoftware kususwa konke, ngoba isithombe sencwadi sivamise ukudluliselwa phambili futhi akukho kulokho okudingeka esicelweni.

Abantwana

Isithombe somntwana singenziwa nge-MatteID ngumzali noma umbheki, okuyinto evamile lapho kufakelwa ingane isicelo sencwadi. Kusebenza imithetho efanayo: isithombe sisala ocingweni futhi asifiki kumuntu.

Izinguquko kulo mgomo

Lo mgomo ugcinwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza, ngakho ushintsha kuphela uma uhlelo lokusebenza luvuselelwa futhi ungakwazi ukufunda inguqulo onayo ngaso sonke isikhathi. Usuku olungenhla lukutshela ukuthi iyiphi inguqulo leyo.
